# Warp Dice — Rules

The complete rules the game runs on. Most are the Rogue Trader rules (Core Rulebook, Into the Storm), condensed. Where the game changes a rule to fit a dice-tray roguelite, the change is marked **adapted**. Every career feature and talent on a sheet carries the same tag: *exact*, *adapted* or *narrative*.

---

## 1. The premise

Your House held a Warrant of Trade for eleven generations. A rival Dynasty boarded your flagship, scuttled it, and dragged the wreck into a **Blackstone Fortress**, a xenos vault older than the Imperium that drifts at the edge of the Koronus Expanse. You are what's left: a **Lord-Captain** with a famous name, an empty treasury, a derelict trader, and two or three loyal retainers.

Survivors have built an **enclave** at the Fortress mouth. They trade with anyone who comes back alive. Few people know the Warrant is gone, so your name still opens doors (your **Profit Factor**), but you need Thrones and valuables from inside the Fortress to buy anything.

**If the Lord-Captain dies, the Dynasty ends and the game is over.**

---

## 2. Founding the Dynasty

1. **Name the House and the lost flagship.**
2. **Build the Lord-Captain** (always a Rogue Trader) through the Origin Path, then choose how to generate Characteristics.
3. **Sign on two or three retainers** from the other seven careers. Each starts from a template; each can be customised the same way as the Lord-Captain.

### 2.1 Characteristics (Core p.14)

There are nine: **WS** (Weapon Skill), **BS** (Ballistic Skill), **S** (Strength), **T** (Toughness), **Ag** (Agility), **Int**, **Per** (Perception), **WP** (Willpower) and **Fel** (Fellowship). A Characteristic's **Bonus** is its tens digit (Toughness 34 → TB 3).

| Method | How |
|---|---|
| 100 points | Each starts at 25; 100 points spread along the career's priorities (max +20 each). Deterministic. |
| Roll 2d10+25 & arrange | Nine rolls, sorted onto the career's priorities. |
| Roll in order | 2d10+25 for each Characteristic in order (the book's method). |
| Heroic | 3d10, drop the lowest, +25, arranged. |

Origin Path modifiers are applied afterwards. Characteristics are clamped to 15–60 at creation.

### 2.2 The Origin Path (Core pp.16–31; Into the Storm pp.9–26)

There are five rows, each six columns wide: **Home World → Birthright → Lure of the Void → Trials and Travails → Motivation**. Each step must sit directly below the previous one, or one column to either side. Into the Storm alternates (✦) share columns with the core entries they replace.

Each entry grants some of: Characteristic modifiers, trained skills, talents, extra Wounds, Fate Points, Profit Factor, Insanity, Corruption, or an heirloom. Many entries offer a choice (for example, "+3 WP or +3 Ag", or the three branches of every Lure). Traits with no mechanical use show on the sheet.

Heirlooms (Core Table 1-2): Archeotech Laspistol, Angevin Era Chainsword, Ancestral Seal, Saint-blessed Carapace, Reliquary of Saint Drusus.

### 2.3 Derived values

- **Wounds** = 2 × TB + 1d5 + origin bonuses + Sound Constitution, +4 for the Lord-Captain. **Adapted:** displayed Wounds are multiplied by 2.5 (VITALITY) and grow 30% per power level, so the Fortune multiplier has room to work.
- **Fate Points**: rolled on the Home World's table (1d10), plus origin and career bonuses. The Lord-Captain gets +1 every venture.
- **Initiative** = 1d10 + Agility Bonus (+ talents and gear).
- **Insanity** and **Corruption** start from the Origin Path.
- **Profit Factor** starts at 12 (a fallen House) plus each explorer's Origin Path adjustments.

## 3. Careers (Core ch. II)

| Career | Fortune talent | Signature options (unlock by Rank) |
|---|---|---|
| **Rogue Trader** | *Speculate*: pay 1 scrip for +1 reroll | Rally (Command: allies Inspired), Concentrate Fire (mark a target), Master Stroke, Claim the Field, Legend of the Expanse |
| **Arch-militant** | *Steady Aim*: nudge a die ±1 | Weapon Master (all weapons, +5), Suppressing Fire, Guarded Stance, Killing Rhythm, Unrelenting |
| **Astropath Transcendent** | *Astral Sight*: swap a die with the next in the bag | Psychic techniques, Psy Rating 2 to 5, Astral Telepathy (+1 tray size), Voice of the Choir |
| **Explorator** | *Fabricate*: copy a die | Analyse Foe, Mechanicus Implants, Rite of Maintenance, Machine Curse |
| **Missionary** | *Sanctify*: turn a die into a Creed 10 | Exhortation, Field Medicae, The Emperor Protects, Litany of Hate, Miracle |
| **Navigator** | *Warp-Sight*: set a die to any value (+1 Corruption) | Navigator Powers (the Lidless Stare and more), Sense the Fortress, Blood of the House |
| **Seneschal** | *Insider Trading*: +1 reroll; extra scrip from Throne dice | Feint, Appraiser (+15% for valuables), Melt into Shadow, Exploit Weakness, Coup de Grâce |
| **Void-master** | *Firing Solution*: flip a die (11 − value) | Evasive Manoeuvres, Void Accustomed, Augur Lock, Call the Guns, Master Gunner |

Each career has the book's **Characteristic Advance scheme** (the cost of the 1st to 4th +5 advance for each Characteristic), a list of career skills and talents, weapon training, and a starting kit (the "lost everything" version of the book kit).

### 3.1 Experience and Rank (Core p.37)

Explorers start with 5,000 xp, 4,500 of it already spent and 500 to spend. Rank thresholds are 5,000 / 7,000 / 10,000 / 13,000 / 17,000 / 21,000 / 25,000 / 30,000. **Adapted:** power level (1–20) = 1 + (xp − 5,000) / 1,250. It drives the Fortress's scaling, Wound growth and damage growth.

**Earning xp:** 40 per fight (80 elite, 150 for the region's lord), plus 900 for clearing a region. Withdrawing keeps the fight xp; a wipe earns nothing. The Drill Deck can sell benched retainers up to 250 xp at a time, but never past a cap a little behind your best crew.

**Spending xp:**

- **Characteristic advance**: +5, at most four per Characteristic, priced by the career scheme.
- **Skill**: trained (100), +10 (200), +20 (300). Career skills only.
- **Talent**: from the career list, once its Rank is reached and its prerequisites are met.
- **Powers**: psychic techniques (Astropaths) or Navigator Powers and their mastery (Adept 300, Master 500).
- **Rank Boon** (Ranks 2, 4, 6, 8): one free career talent.

## 4. Tests (Core p.230)

Roll d100. Rolling **equal to or under** the target (Characteristic or Skill + modifiers) passes. Each full 10 of margin is a **Degree of Success** (or of Failure). 01 always passes; 100 always fails. **Doubles** (11, 22…) matter for psykers.

Difficulty modifiers: Easy +30, Routine +20, Ordinary +10, Challenging +0, Difficult −10, Hard −20, Very Hard −30.

**Skills:** a trained skill tests its full Characteristic (+10 or +20 with mastery). An untrained **Basic** skill tests half the Characteristic; an untrained **Advanced** skill cannot be used. Lore skills are folded into one **Forbidden Lore** skill.

**Situational modifiers** on all Tests: Inspired/Blessed +10, Frightened −10, Fatigued −10, Poisoned −10.

## 5. Combat (Core ch. IX)

### 5.1 Turns

Initiative is 1d10 + Agility Bonus. On their turn an explorer has a **Full Action or two Half Actions**, plus free actions and one Fortune play. Defensive Stance and Wall of Steel grant extra Reactions.

### 5.2 Ranks

**Adapted:** range bands become front and back ranks.

- Melee reaches the enemy front rank, or anyone once the front rank is down.
- Shooting the back rank is −10 (Long Range), unless you have Marksman or a scope.
- A pistol fired from the front rank at the front rank is +10 (Point Blank).
- Basic and heavy weapons fired from the front rank while engaged are −20, unless bayoneted.

### 5.3 Attack actions (Table 9-4)

| Action | Cost | Modifier | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard Attack | Half | +0 | One attack. |
| Semi-Auto Burst | Full | +10 | +1 hit per two Degrees of Success (up to RoF − 1). |
| Full-Auto Burst | Full | +20 | +1 hit per Degree of Success (up to RoF − 1). |
| Called Shot | Full | −20 (−10 with Deadeye Shot / Sure Strike) | Hits the head; the hit is a guaranteed Righteous Fury (**adapted**). |
| All Out Attack | Full | +20 WS | No Dodge or Parry until your next turn. |
| Charge | Full | +10 WS | Moves you to the front rank. |
| Multiple Attacks | Full | — | One Test per swing allowed by Swift/Lightning Attack and an off-hand weapon (Two-Weapon Wielder, Gunslinger). |
| Aim | Half | +10 (+20 if repeated) | Applies to the next attack. |
| Defensive Stance | Full | — | +1 Reaction, foes −20 WS against you, and Ward (temporary Wounds) from your Fortune hand. |

Other attack modifiers: stunned or snared target +20, prone target (+10 melee / −10 ranged), Frenzy +10 WS, Hatred (Xenos) +10 WS, Bloodtracker +20 against a foe below half Wounds, untrained weapon −20, off hand −20 (without Ambidextrous).

### 5.4 Hits, locations and damage

- **Hit location** (Table 9-6): reverse the d100. 01–10 head, 11–20 right arm, 21–30 left arm, 31–70 body, 71–85 right leg, 86–00 left leg. Extra hits from a burst roll their own locations.
- **Damage** = weapon dice + bonus (melee adds Strength Bonus; Crushing Blow / Mighty Shot +2; Best melee +1). Tearing rolls an extra die and drops the lowest.
- **Armour and Toughness.** **Adapted:** at this game's scale, Armour and Toughness become a soak fraction: 4% per point of AP the weapon fails to penetrate (AP − Pen), plus 2.5% per point of TB, capped at 60%. Warp damage ignores it. Penetration still decides what bites: a melta (Pen 13) shrugs off carapace, a lasgun (Pen 0) doesn't.
- **Righteous Fury** (Core p.245): every damage die showing 10 (9 or 10 with an Augmetic Eye relic) can become a Fury. Confirm it by passing the attack Test again, then add 1d10 (and again on another 10).
- **Weapon qualities**: Accurate, Reliable/Unreliable, Tearing, Balanced/Unbalanced/Unwieldy (affect Parry), Shocking (Toughness Test or Stunned), Toxic (Toughness −10 or Poisoned), Flame (hits automatically; Agility Test or catch fire; spreads along the rank), Blast (spills to another foe in the rank), Scatter (+10 at the front rank), Storm (double hits), Overheat (91+ burns the wielder), Snare, Defensive (shields: +2 AP on body and arm, +10 Parry).
- **Jams** (Core p.111): Common ranged weapons jam on 96+, Reliable only on 100, Unreliable or Poor on 91+, Best never. Clearing a jam is a Full Action (Half with Rapid Reload, free once a turn with Technical Knock).

### 5.5 Reactions

Once per round you may **Dodge** (Dodge skill, or half Agility untrained) or **Parry** (Weapon Skill with a melee weapon, modified by Balanced/Unbalanced/Defensive/Unwieldy) to negate a hit. All Out Attack and Frenzy forbid reactions. Counter Attack strikes back after a successful Parry. Force fields roll their rating on d100 to negate a hit. Foes with a Dodge rating (skirmishers, Eldar) can Dodge one hit a round.

### 5.6 Wounds, Blood Loss and death

- At 0 Wounds an explorer falls. **Adapted:** each turn they Test Toughness against **Blood Loss**. Three passes and they stabilise; three failures (or Critical Damage taken while down) and they die. A natural 01 sees them rise with 1 Wound. Die Hard re-rolls the Test; True Grit halves Critical Damage taken while down.
- **Fate Points** refresh every venture (Core p.233). **Spend** one for +2 rerolls or to recover 1d5 Wounds (scaled). Void born roll 1d10 and keep the point on a 9 (*Charmed*). **Burn** one when you would die: you survive, stable, and lose that point for good. Burning is automatic for anyone with Fate left.
- **The Lord-Captain's death ends the game**, in both Legacy and Story modes. In Legacy mode the dead stay dead. In Story mode fallen retainers are dragged home alive.

### 5.7 Fear, Insanity and Corruption

- **Fear** (Core p.315): the first time a fearsome foe appears (Fear 1–4), every explorer Tests Willpower at −10 per rating above 1. Iron Discipline (+10 to the party), Resistance (Fear) (+10) and Unshakeable Faith (re-roll) help; Into the Jaws of Hell grants immunity. Failure: Frightened for 2 rounds and 1d5 Insanity.
- **Insanity** comes from Fear, Psychic Phenomena and events. The Chirurgeon's tent treats it at 10 points per visit.
- **Corruption** comes from the warp, Navigator Warp-Sight and xenos bargains. Armour of Contempt halves it.

### 5.8 Psychic techniques (Core ch. VI)

**Focus Power Test** = Willpower + 5 × effective Psy Rating (+ focus gear and talents). A result of 91+ always fails.

| Strength | Effective Psy Rating | Psychic Phenomena |
|---|---|---|
| Fettered | half, rounded up | never (sanctioned psykers) |
| Unfettered | full | on doubles |
| Push | +3 (+4 with Voice of the Choir) | always, +15 on the table |

**Techniques available:**

- **Telekinesis**: Force Bolt, Telekinetic Crush, Telekinetic Shield, Storm of Force.
- **Telepathy**: Compel, Dominate, Terrify, Psychic Scream, Inspire, Mind Probe.
- **Divination**: Foreshadow, In Harm's Way, Augury.

Opposed techniques are resisted by the target's Willpower: the psyker must pass with more Degrees of Success. Constructs and daemons ignore mind-affecting powers. **Psychic Phenomena** (d100): Dark Foreboding, Hoarfrost (+1 Insanity), Unnatural Aura (+1 Corruption), Psychic Drain, Warp Echo (the tray flips), Ghostly Voices, or **Perils of the Warp**: The Gibbering (Stunned, 1d10 Insanity), Warp Burn, Psychic Backlash (Psy Rating 0 for the fight), or a **Daemonic Incursion** (a warp entity joins the fight).

### 5.9 Navigator Powers (Core ch. VII)

Navigator Powers test Willpower or Perception, at +0 / +10 / +20 for Novice / Adept / Master. They never cause Phenomena.

- **The Lidless Stare** (every Navigator starts with it): opposed Willpower against every living foe. Deals 1d10 + WPB Energy (2d10 at Adept) ignoring armour, and Stuns. At Master, non-boss victims must pass a Toughness −10 Test or die. It costs the Navigator Fatigue.
- **Held in My Gaze**: sustained; the target is helpless.
- **Foreshadowing**: allies are Foreseen (+Dodge/Parry).
- **A Cloud in the Warp**: foes −20 to hit the party.
- **Gaze into the Abyss**: marks a foe and makes it Vulnerable; out of combat, finds hidden passages.
- **The Course Untravelled**: out of combat, reveals the way to the region's lord.
- **Tides of Time and Space**: an ally gains an extra Fortune play.

## 6. The Fortune tray (original to this game)

- **The bag.** Each venture starts with a fresh bag: 6 **iron** d10s plus two d10s per explorer in their career's material (Aquila, Adamant, Warp, Cog, Creed, Eye, Ledger, Void).
- **The tray.** Each fight draws 7 dice (+1 at power levels 5, 11 and 17; +1 with Astral Telepathy). On an explorer's turn: reroll selected dice (2 rerolls per turn, more from talents, gear and relics), then play **up to five dice with an action**. Unplayed dice stay for the next explorer. A missed attack returns the dice.
- **Hands.** High Die, Pair, Two Pair, Three of a Kind, Straight, **Colours** (five of one material), Full House, Four of a Kind, Straight Colours, Five of a Kind, Full Colours, **Emperor's Hand** (five of a kind in one material). Only the dice that make the pattern score. Iron and loaded dice are colourless; xenos dice count as every colour.
- **Score** = (action damage + hand chips + scored pips) × mult. Dice matching the actor's material add +1 mult. **Every scored 10 is a Righteous Fury**: +1d10 to the action's damage before the multiplier. Heals and Ward scale on a gentler curve. For opposed powers and Machine Curse, the hand sharpens the Test instead (+5 per two mult, up to +30).
- **Special dice and materials**:
  - Throne: +1 scrip when scored.
  - Blackstone: ×2 mult, 1 in 4 chance to crumble.
  - Xenos: counts as every material.
  - Loaded: rolls twice and keeps the higher face.
  - Cryo: +8 chips.
  - Named dice: Heretic, Gambler's, Cogitator, Tithe, Relic, Frenzon, Warp-Touched.
- **Face sanctifications.** Gilded, Keen, Fierce, Savage, Wild, Blessed. Foes can apply Tainted faces.
- **Relics** (64, five slots). They score left to right, so order matters.
- **Tarot cards** level up hand types. **Tokens**: Litany of Recalibration, Tarot Card, Emperor's Favour, Stimm Shot, Medi-Injector.
- **Fortune Rank** rises after each fight: pick a boon. Every third rank is a milestone that offers a structural upgrade.
- **Scrip** is the Fortress's run-only coin. It earns interest (+1 per 5 held, max 5) and buys from Fortress scavengers.
- **Hexes** and **curses**: foes steal, jinx, taint or invert dice. Elites and region lords bring curses that bend the tray for the whole fight.
- Everything on the tray is lost when the venture ends. The Fortress never lets anything out the same way twice.

## 7. Ventures into the Fortress

- **Party:** the Lord-Captain plus one to three retainers.
- **Regions** unlock in order: The Shattered Quays → The Xenos Arboretum → The Drowned Reliquary → The Obsidian Labyrinth → The Gravitic Heart. Each has a meter:

| Region | Meter | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Shattered Quays | Decompression | Breached rooms raise it. At 3+, the party bleeds, but Void and Aquila dice gain +1 mult. |
| Xenos Arboretum | Bloom | Rises as you move. At 3+, foes regenerate, but Ledger and Eye dice gain +4 chips. |
| Drowned Reliquary | Coolant | Pump levers toggle it. Low opens flooded passages; high gives Cryo and Void dice +2 mult, but foes resist Energy. |
| Obsidian Labyrinth | Reflection | Mirrored halls swap Left and Right. At 3, phase-doors appear. |
| Gravitic Heart | Gravity | Rotates your directions (void-born and Void-masters keep their bearings), and gives +1 reroll. |

- **The Fortress shifts.** Every venture is a new seeded labyrinth. The same seed gives the same layout.
- **Rooms:** fights, elites, salvage, events, scavengers, black altars, safe chambers, the warp-key, and the region's lord behind a sealed hatch.
- **Events** are Skill Tests. The best explorer for the skill rolls, at a difficulty that rises with power level (−5 per tier). Some checks have a **helper**: an Explorator or a Combi-tool for tech, a psyker or Navigator for the warp, Medicae, an Auspex, the Lord-Captain's name, or faith. A helper turns the check into an automatic success. Every event has an option with no check.
- **Search** uses Search or Awareness to find hidden passages and loose Thrones.
- **Safe chambers:** *Bind wounds* recovers about a third of everyone's Wounds (more if a Medicae Test passes), once per chamber. *Make camp* fully heals the party once per venture, but the Fortress grows harder (+1 depth).
- **Leaving:** clear the region's lord and walk out to bank all carried Thrones and items, plus xp. Withdrawing from the entrance keeps half the Thrones, all items, and the fight xp. A wipe loses everything carried.

## 8. The enclave

- **Thrones** are banked coin. **Profit Factor** is your standing.
- **Market** (stock changes daily): goods rarer than Common need an **Acquisition Test** (**adapted** from Core p.274):

  > Profit Factor + Availability modifier + Craftsmanship modifier + 30 (a single personal item) + one fifth of your best trader's Commerce.

  - Availability modifiers run from Ubiquitous +70 down to Unique −70.
  - Craftsmanship modifiers: Poor +10, Good −10, Best −30.
  - Each Degree of Success knocks 5% off the price, up to 30%.
  - If you fail, that seller won't deal with you again that day.
  - You always pay the price in Thrones.
- **Exchange:** sell valuables at full value (+15% with a Seneschal's Appraiser feature). Valuables worth 150+ can be **presented** to the elders for +1 Profit Factor per 300 Thrones of value. Other gear sells for 40%.
- **Craftsmanship** (Core p.112):

  | Grade | Ranged | Melee | Armour |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Poor | Unreliable | −10 to attack | −10 Dodge |
  | Good | Reliable | +5 to attack | +1 AP |
  | Best | Never jams | +10 to attack, +1 damage | +1 AP |

  The **Tech-Shrine** raises Craftsmanship and fits upgrades and special ammunition: Mono, Red-Dot, Scope, Motion Predictor, Man-Stopper, Inferno, Tempest, Hot-Shot, Bayonet, Sanctified, Serrated, Relic-Wrought.
- **Facilities** (Thrones plus warp-keystones from region lords): Muster Hall, Armsmonger's Row, Chirurgeon's Tent, Tech-Shrine, Drill Deck.
- **Retainers** can be signed on for Thrones. They are free while your crew is too thin to venture out.
- Clearing a region gives +1 Profit Factor and a keystone, and unlocks the next region.

## 9. The story and the finale

Clearing each region for the first time reveals a chapter of what happened to your flagship. Once all five regions are cleared and the Lord-Captain reaches **power level 18** (Rank 7), the **Heart of the Fortress** opens.

The Heart is a long venture through the Gravitic Heart. At its end waits **Lord-Captain Rathbone, the Usurper**: three phases, fused to the Fortress, wielding the *Blackstone Dominion* curse. Defeat him to reclaim the Warrant of Trade (+10 Profit Factor) and read the epilogue.

## 10. Balance notes

`npm run sim -- <runs> <level> <zone|finale> <difficulty>` plays headless ventures with the autoplayer. At the last calibration, standard ventures won 88–96% at power levels 1, 8, 15 and 20, and the finale won about 90%. Per venture that is generous. Over a campaign of ~20 ventures, one lost Lord-Captain ends everything, so a real Dynasty's survival odds are much lower. Withdraw when a venture turns bad.
